Features

#1 Android

Plug and Mirror

Connect an Android phone by USB with USB debugging on, and its screen appears in seconds.

#2 iPhone

Cable or AirPlay

Mirror an iPhone over its USB cable with video and sound, or over AirPlay on your Wi-Fi.

#3 GPU

Hardware Decoding

Video decodes on the graphics card with D3D11VA, with a software fallback when it cannot.

#4 OBS Soon

Clean Capture Window

A borderless window with only the phone picture, made for OBS scenes.

#5 Record Soon

MP4 Recording

Save what is on the phone screen straight to an MP4 file.

#6 Private

Fully Offline

No account, no telemetry, no update checks. Tezca never talks to a server.

How It Works

  1. 01

    Install

    Run Tezca Setup. No account, nothing to download.

  2. 02

    Connect a phone

    Plug in an Android phone or an iPhone, or pick Tezca under Screen Mirroring.

  3. 03

    Capture in OBS

    Add the Tezca window to a scene as a Window Capture source.

No Account.
No Telemetry.
No Cloud.

Offline
Tezca never contacts a server. Phones connect over the USB cable or your own Wi-Fi.

Local data
Settings and logs stay on your PC, in %APPDATA%\PhoneMirror.

No updates behind your back
There are no update checks. A new version arrives only when you download it.

Requirements

PC

  • Windows 10 (1809 or newer) or Windows 11, 64-bit (x64)
  • A graphics card with DirectX 11
  • 500 MB of free disk space

Phones

  • Android: a USB cable and USB debugging turned on
  • iPhone or iPad by USB cable: Apple Devices and a one-time capture filter setup
  • iPhone or iPad over AirPlay: the same Wi-Fi network as the PC

FAQ

Yes. Tezca is free, with no paid tier, no trial and no account.

No. Tezca works fully offline. Phones connect over the USB cable or your local Wi-Fi, and Tezca never contacts a server, not even to check for updates.

Early builds are not code-signed yet, so Windows does not recognise the publisher. Check the SHA-256 of the file against the one on the download page, then choose More info › Run anyway.

On the phone, open Settings › About phone and tap Build number seven times. Then open Settings › System › Developer options and turn on USB debugging. When you plug the phone in, tap Allow on the prompt.

For AirPlay, the iPhone and the PC must be on the same Wi-Fi, the network must be set to Private in Windows, and Windows Firewall must allow Tezca's AirPlay receiver. Guest networks often block it. Or skip Wi-Fi and use the USB cable.
